## Major Projects

<!-- Format: - **Title** — Affiliation. One-sentence description. -->

- **Crowdsourced data-based driver behavior modeling for traffic routing control** — Mitsubishi Electric Research Labs (research intern). Probabilistic modeling of personalized driving behavior from historical driving data; SUMO-based validation.
- **Robotic manipulation of uncured composite structures for aerospace** — Boeing Advanced Research Center. Safe manipulation of flexible structures with multiple cooperative robot arms for precise molding of deformable objects.
- **Vision and lidar based autonomous tracking and formation control** — Precision Controls Lab, UW Seattle. Cooperative maneuvering of a decentralized TurtleBot3 platoon around obstacles using RealSense, 360° lidar and time-of-flight sensors.
- **Longitudinal control of connected autonomous vehicles** — Precision Controls Lab, UW Seattle. Safe platooning with small inter-vehicle spacing, without V2V communication, under delays, with string-stability guarantees.
- **Cohesive transitions in networks in the presence of delays** — Precision Controls Lab, UW Seattle. Stability and performance theory with novel distributed network control techniques.
