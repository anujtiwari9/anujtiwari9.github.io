## Prospective students

We work on robot learning for manipulation, multi-robot coordination, warehouse automation and networked control. Students with a background in mechanical, electrical, computer science, aerospace or related areas are welcome to apply.

Admissions to the Ph.D., M.S. by Research, M.Tech. and dual-degree programs at IIT Madras go through the institute's official admission process. Please apply through that process first. Writing to us does not replace it.

**Before you email**, please include:

- a short note on which of our research themes interests you and why
- your CV and a link to any code or projects
- the program you are applying to, and your expected start date

## Undergraduate and project students

IIT Madras students interested in short projects can email with their department, year, and the topic they would like to try.

## Industry and academic partners

We seek collaborations on engineering problems in manufacturing, recycling and remanufacturing, and warehouse logistics. If you have a problem that needs teams of robots, get in touch.
